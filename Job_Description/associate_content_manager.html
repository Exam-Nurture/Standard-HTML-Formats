About the Role
As an Associate Content Manager, you will be a key driver in the content engine of ExamNurture. You will work directly with AI tools and internal workflows to produce high-quality study materials, test series, and digital content for thousands of competitive exam aspirants.
Training Note: Comprehensive training will be provided to freshers to bring them up to speed with our internal workflows and AI-driven content generation tools.
Key Responsibilities
1. PYQ Generation
  • Generate Previous Year Questions (PYQ) using advanced AI tools.
  • Verify accuracy, formatting, and mathematical notations of questions.
  • Categorize questions by subject, chapter, and difficulty levels.
  • Ensure delivery against strict weekly production targets.
2. Test Series Generation
  • Create high-quality mock tests and comprehensive test series.
  • Ensure balanced difficulty levels and syllabus coverage.
  • Upload and manage test series across various digital platforms.
  • Review user feedback for continuous quality improvements.
3. PDF & PPT Creation
  • Design and create study PDFs and interactive presentation PPTs.
  • Maintain strict brand consistency across all visual materials.
  • Use tools like Gamma, Canva, and PowerPoint for professional output.
  • Organize and maintain a repository of reusable content assets.
4. YT Videos to Telegram
  • Extract key takeaways and summarize content from YouTube videos.
  • Manage daily Telegram quiz updates to keep the community engaged.
  • Coordinate video upload schedules with the production team.
  • Distribute course links and study materials across Telegram channels.
5. Data Labeling
  • Label and tag content for AI training and search engine optimization.
  • Maintain data integrity and consistency across all internal platforms.
  • Collaborate with the tech team for data structuring requirements.
  • Audit labeled data to ensure high accuracy for machine learning models.
6. Typist / Data Entry
  • Perform rapid and extremely accurate data entry tasks.
  • Type and format multi-lingual content for courses and exams.
  • Handle document digitization, cleanup, and OCR correction.
  • Meet strict deadlines for large-scale data entry projects.
