About the Role
This isn't a theory-only internship. At ExamNurture, you will work exclusively at the intersection of Python-driven engineering and Applied AI. You will be responsible for building functional AI POCs, developing inference APIs using FastAPI, and optimizing content generation pipelines that directly power the student experience.
Key Responsibility Zones
Python-First Backend (FastAPI)
Zone 1
Build and maintain high-performance REST APIs using FastAPI focused on serving AI models. You will create endpoints for synchronous and asynchronous model inference, ensuring fast response times for production features.
AI / ML Proof of Concept (POC)
Zone 2
Develop working demos using text, voice, and image models.
  • Text: Auto-MCQ generation, summarization logic.
  • Voice: Audio-to-structured note conversion.
  • Image: Question extraction from scans/PDFs using OCR/VLM.
Prompt Engineering & SLMs
Zone 3
Write, test, and iterate prompts for LLMs and small language models (SLMs). You will focus on "Domain-Specific" accuracy for Indian competitive exams, ensuring the AI outputs perfectly follow pedagogical standards.
Automation & Pipeline Engineering
Zone 4
Deploy Python scripts to automate content tagging, categorization, and formatting. You will build internal utilities that use AI to reduce manual effort in large-scale content production.
