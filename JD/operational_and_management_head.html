About the Role
You will be the operational spine of ExamNurture — the single point of coordination between the three founders and the entire execution layer. The founders own strategy and vision; you make sure it gets done, on time, at quality. This is a high-ownership role that touches every function of the business: platforms, content, teachers, digital media, and administration.
Key Responsibilities
Platform & App Operations
  • Own daily operations across two external-facing platforms:
  • HJ/EN App — manage content uploads (video courses, test series, PDFs), course publications, and coupon creation via the app backend.
  • ExamNurture Website (examnurture.com) — manage course listings, link creation, and content uploads via the website backend.
  • Keep both platforms updated, error-free, and in sync with team production output.
Note: The internal team portal used by all members is managed by the Tech Lead. You will use it as a team member only.
Content Pipeline Management
  • Oversee PYQ and Test Series generation by the content team.
  • Ensure timelines, quality checks, and delivery against weekly targets.
  • Supervise PDF and PPT creation output from the team.
Teacher & Faculty Coordination
  • Fulfill teacher requirements in consultation with the Academic Head.
  • Maintain teacher schedules and coordinate live classes.
  • Ensure subject coverage is always in place and gaps are escalated early.
Digital & Social Media Execution
  • Manage YT and Instagram video upload schedules via the Social Media Handler.
  • Ensure daily Telegram quiz updates go out without gaps.
  • Coordinate YT/App live class streaming logistics.
Company Administration
  • Handle day-to-day company management tasks — tracking, follow-ups, cross-team coordination.
  • Ensure nothing falls through the cracks across all three founders' work streams.
  • Act as the first point of contact for operational issues before they reach the founders.

  • ◆Extremely efficient in managing and organizing parallel tasks and coordinating across multiple teams seamlessly.
